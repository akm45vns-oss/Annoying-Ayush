# Ayush Voice Pack Directory (100% Free)

You can drop your own recorded `.mp3` or `.m4a` audio files directly into this directory!

### Supported Audio Files:
- `kiss.mp3`: Spoken when Shristy taps Kiss 💋
- `hug.mp3`: Spoken when Shristy taps Hug 🧸
- `blush.mp3`: Spoken when Shristy taps Blush 😳
- `cuddle.mp3`: Spoken when Shristy taps Cuddle 🌸
- `laugh.mp3`: Spoken when Shristy taps Tease / Laugh 😜
- `loveNote.mp3`: Spoken when Shristy opens a Love Note 💌
- `greeting.mp3`: Spoken on initial greeting ☀️

### Alternative: In-Game Recording
You can also record these voice notes directly from your phone microphone inside the **Personalize Settings (⚙️)** modal without touching any files!
